## 2. Logo

The mark echoes how *The Doubling* begins: a **point of light with rings whose radii double**
(¼ → ½ → 1 of the outer ring). The outer ring is left **open**, and a small **cyan dot** sits at its
end: *you are here*. Its colouring runs from lavender to cyan across the mark, as if lit from the upper left.

- **Files:** `lumiera_brand/assets/logo/` holds the mark (transparent, on dark, white mono), the
  LUMIERA wordmark (white, dark) and lockups (horizontal, stacked).
  - Regenerate any size with `lumiera_brand/build_brand.py`.
  - Code that draws the mark MUST use the geometry in `lumiera_brand/brandlib.py` (`MARK`, `draw_mark`).
    Never retype it, eyeball it or trace it.
  - Over an image or another background, use the `*_transparent` files. The `*_on_dark` files are
    finished plates with their own Space-glow background, which shows as a rectangle on Deep Space.
    Use them only on their own, as uploads.
- **Where it appears:**
  - Profile pictures, the YouTube banner and watermark, the *Start here* highlight, and stills
    outside the films (collaborations, press).
  - **Never** as a sign-off inside a film (`film-style.md` §1), and never on a cover (a cover is a film frame).
  - A film MAY show the mark's geometry as story content (e.g. the open rings and the cyan dot drawn as
    an object in the story), built from `MARK`. The faint full-circle warp rings of `film-style.md` §3
    are a film device, not the mark, and are not built from `MARK`.
  - Use the stacked lockup in square-ish spaces and the horizontal one in wide spaces.
- **Wordmark:** "LUMIERA" in Inter Display Medium, capitals, with 0.32 em of space between letters
  (none after the last letter, so it stays centred).
- **Lockups** (mark outer-ring diameter = H): wordmark cap height 0.3 H; ≈ 0.34 H of space between the
  outer ring and the wordmark (generated by `build_brand.py` `lockup()`).
- **Clear space:** keep a quarter of the outer ring's diameter empty around the mark, measured from
  the outer ring's edge, not from its glow.
- **Minimum size:** 32 px outer-ring diameter for the full-colour mark (a 47 px square mark PNG, since
  the ring fills 0.68 of the canvas). Below that, use the white mono mark.
- **Backgrounds:** deep space or any dark, calm background. On light backgrounds, use the wordmark
  in Deep Space and no glowing mark.
- **MUST NOT:** recolour single rings, close the open ring, move or remove the cyan dot, stretch it,
  or add shadows, outlines or bevels. This applies to every full-colour mark. The one exception is the
  white mono mark (below the minimum size, and the YouTube watermark): it is drawn with
  `draw_mark(mono=…)` in a single colour and has no you-are-here dot. Never add one by hand.

## 4. Type

| Role | Font | Case |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Inter Display Medium, 0.32 em letter spacing | CAPS, only for "LUMIERA" |
| On-screen titles | Inter Display Bold | **CAPS** ("THE MILKY WAY") |
| Hook question | Inter Display Bold | Sentence case |
| On-screen lines, captions, bios, descriptions | Inter Medium / Regular | **Sentence case** |
| Numbers, counters, units, step chips | JetBrains Mono | "STEP 116", "1.7 × 10⁰ m" |

- **Powers of ten:** write them as "1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m" (a multiplication sign and a raised exponent) in
  every caption, description, asset and frame. Never use `^`, e-notation or "x". In JetBrains Mono,
  draw the exponent as raised, smaller digits, as the HUD readout does, because the font has no
  superscript minus. Static film text that needs an exponent (a stop's size text) is set in Inter
  Medium with Unicode superscripts instead (`film-style.md` §5).
- **Fonts:** Inter 4.0 or later (the Inter Display family exists only from 4.0) and JetBrains Mono 2.x.
  Both are free and open-source. Don't substitute other fonts. Renderers replace a missing family
  without warning, so check the fonts are installed before rendering.

## 5. Voice

Calm, precise, awe-filled. The scale does the shouting; we don't.

1. **Open an honest loop and close it in the same video.** Ask a question the viewer can answer in
   their head. Never hold the answer back for "part 2" or a follow.
2. **Give the guess room.** Ask, then let the counter run.
3. **Numbers are sacred.** Every figure comes from the film's sizes table. A figure the film shows is
   written the same way in the caption, title and comments; lists of sizes quote the table
   (`film-style.md` §6). Round as in `film-style.md` §6: to the nearest value, with
   "about" or "≈" when a figure is more than ~2% off. Never round because a number sounds bigger.
4. **Keep the qualifiers:** *observable* universe, *where known physics runs out*, *on average*,
   *in theory*. Flag any simplification in one plain line. An outro MAY use a poetic phrase ("to the
   edge of everything") once the qualified term has been on screen. Captions, titles, descriptions
   and comments use the qualified term.
5. **Short declarative sentences**, mostly present tense. **Banned:** "mind-blowing", "insane",
   "you won't believe", "wait for it", fake urgency, exclamation marks.
6. **Comment prompts have a real answer:** a guess, a prediction, the next thing to measure. Never
   "only 1% get this", and never tease what the video doesn't deliver.
7. **"You" is the measuring stick**, not someone to shout at ("Doubling 116. Remember that.").
   Address the viewer at most once or twice per caption.
8. **Punctuation:** use `·` and line breaks. **No emoji anywhere** (captions, bios, comments, alt
   text, titles, on screen). The only marks are one **✦** per text (the house mark) and **↓**, used
   only to point at a link. Titles and on-screen text carry neither; on-screen punctuation follows
   `film-style.md` §5.
9. **Promise only what the channel keeps.** A cadence line ("A new film every week") appears only
   while that schedule is real; otherwise leave it out everywhere.
10. **Correct in public.** If a number is wrong, pin a short correction with the right figure and a
    source, and thank whoever caught it. Never argue, never delete.

**Examples**
- ✅ "Start at the Planck length, where known physics runs out. Double it. Then again."
- ✅ "Lock in your guess: how many doublings to a galaxy? One number."
- ❌ "This will BLOW YOUR MIND 🤯 Wait for the end!!!"
- ❌ "The smallest length in physics." (It's a misconception; say "where known physics runs out".)

## 7. Content pillars

1. **Scale · How big is that?** The signature series, which *The Doubling* starts. Sizes, distances,
   durations and quantities made visible one step at a time, with step chips, a climbing counter and
   copies of a smaller neighbour lining up. Post it most often early on.
2. **Cosmos & Light · How far, how fast?** Space and the physics of light: how light travels,
   scatters and interferes, and where things really sit in the sky.
3. **Life, Measured · How many of you?** Biology at its true scale: cells, DNA, blood and growth,
   counted and laid out. Estimate ranges shown honestly.
4. **Pure Patterns · Why does that work?** Math made visible: probability, geometry and number
   patterns drawn as light, with one surprising answer per film.

**Instagram highlights** are *Start here* plus one per pillar, in this order and with these names:
Start here · Scale · Cosmos · Life · Patterns. A topic inside a pillar (e.g. light) goes in that
pillar's highlight. Add a highlight only when a pillar is added; its icon is drawn in
`lumiera_brand/build_brand.py` in the same line-of-light style.

Each new idea MUST fit one pillar and the film structure in `film-style.md` §2. The idea backlog,
with checked figures, is in `ideas.md`.
